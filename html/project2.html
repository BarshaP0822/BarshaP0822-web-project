Sugar- The sweet culprit

Sugar is composed of 12 carbon atoms, 22 hydrogen atoms, and 11 oxygen atoms. Sugar is present in almost all the processed

food including food advertised as "healthy" food.

There are over 60+ names for sugar and some of the common name is sucrose, high-frutose corn syrup, fructose, maltose etc.

Since I had my baby, I have been reading food labels and ingredients to see if I can avoid as much sugar as I can.

But food manufacturer uses different names for sugar in their labels and often use terms like "fruit juice", "natural sugar"

to make it sound like they are healthy.

I believe sugar is sugar no matter if it comes from sugar cane or fresh fruits, and our body will process it the same way.

I try my best to limit the amount of processed sugar in my daughter's diet and try to avoid any product with added sugar.

Why is sugar hard to avoid

  • There are over 60+ names for sugar
  • Reading the ingredents in the food lable are not always easy
  • They comes in different form like natural sugar, maple syrup
  • Manufacturer used different names for same sugar that we dont recognize
